# Changelog

All notable changes to `@nera-static/theme-stack` are documented in this file. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/), and the theme follows [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/) over its contract (see the README).

## [1.0.0] - Unreleased

### Added

-   The "Stack" blog theme for people who write about code: `views/`, `assets/css/theme.css`, `config/theme.yaml` and self-hosted webfonts (Bricolage Grotesque, Inter, JetBrains Mono under `assets/fonts/stack/`), presentation only — no JavaScript, no third-party request.
