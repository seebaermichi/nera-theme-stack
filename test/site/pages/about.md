I am Jonas, a backend developer in Leipzig. I have built Laravel applications for ten years, the last two with language models somewhere in the request cycle. This blog is where I write down what broke.

## What you will find here

- Working code, tested against the versions named in each post.
- Numbers from production instead of benchmarks from a laptop.
- Corrections. When a post turns out wrong, it gets an update note at the top.

> If it only works in the demo, it does not go on the blog.

## Get in touch

The fastest way is [Mastodon](https://mastodon.social/@example). For longer questions, write to [hello@example.com](mailto:hello@example.com). I do not take sponsored posts, and the `composer.json` of every example is in the [repository](https://github.com/seebaermichi/nera-theme-stack).
